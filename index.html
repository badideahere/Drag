A real driveline

Engine rpm comes from wheel speed through the gears when the clutch is locked, and from the crank's own inertia when it is not. Nothing teleports. Select second at 110 mph and the wheels will drag the engine past the limiter whether it likes it or not.

Damage you caused

Engine, gearbox, clutch and tire health are tracked separately and persist until you pay to fix them. Nothing fails at random — every point of wear traces back to something you did with your right foot.

Three ways to drive

Arcade handles the clutch and gears for you on the same physics. Manual gives you the shifter. Realistic gives you all three pedals, and the ability to stall it on the startline in front of everyone.

Honest upgrades

Every part changes simulation variables — cam timing shifts the torque curve, a bigger turbo changes spool and lag, forged rods raise the rpm the engine survives. The dyno graph is produced by the same code that drives the car.

Opponents who drive

The AI runs its own vehicle through the same physics with its own gearing, launch rpm and temperament. It can bog, spin the tires, shift early or red-light. There is no speed multiplier anywhere.

Progress that follows you

Money, cars, upgrades, tunes, wear and best times live in the cloud. Log in from anywhere and your garage is exactly as you left it.
